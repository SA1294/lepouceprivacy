Le Pouce – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Le Pouce is a mobile game for iOS and Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. In short: while you play, the app collects nothing, sends nothing, and shares nothing. The only exception is an optional donation to the developer: if you choose to make one, the purchase is handled by Apple or Google, and recorded by our payment provider, RevenueCat (see section 2).

1. No data collection while you play

Le Pouce does not collect, sell, or share any personal data while you play. The app has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools. It does not use the internet at all while you play.

2. Optional donations (RevenueCat)

The “Following” tab at the top of the feed lets you make an optional donation to the developer. A donation unlocks nothing. If you never open this screen, nothing in this section applies to you.

  • Payment. The payment itself is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your name, email address, card number or any other payment details.
  • RevenueCat. To process and check donations, the app uses RevenueCat, a payment service provider. RevenueCat is only contacted when you open the donation screen, never while you play. It receives the details of the purchase (which donation, price, currency, date, the store's purchase receipt) and a random anonymous identifier created by the app. This identifier is not linked to your name, email address or any account.
  • Use. This information is used only to complete and verify your donation, and to give us anonymous totals (for example, how many donations were made). It is not used for advertising or to track you across other apps or websites.
  • More information. RevenueCat handles this data under its own privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

3. Information kept only on your device

A few things are used only on your phone, and never leave it:

  • Age group. When you first open the app, it asks for your age group (for example 13–17 or 18–24). This answer is saved on your device only, to adapt the content and the comparison figures shown at the end of a game. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app.
  • Battery level. The app may read your battery level and charging status on the device to display a short in-game message. This information is not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Language and region. The app reads your phone's language and region settings to choose the language of the game and the comparison figures shown at the end. Your language choice is saved on your device only.

4. Sharing your result

At the end of a game, you can choose to share an image of your result. The image is created on your device and handed to your phone's standard share menu. Nothing is shared unless you choose to do so, and the app does not see where you send it.

5. Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

6. App stores

Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) may collect information when you download the app, such as download counts, under their own privacy policies. We only receive the general download statistics they provide.

7. Children

Le Pouce is intended for people aged 13 and over. The app collects no personal data while you play. A donation is processed as described in section 2, with an anonymous identifier only.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
