Le Pouce – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Le Pouce is a mobile game for iOS and Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. In short: the app collects nothing, sends nothing, and shares nothing.

1. No data collection

Le Pouce does not collect, store on a server, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking tools and no server. It does not use the internet at all while you play.

2. Information kept only on your device

A few things are used only on your phone, and never leave it:

  • Age group. When you first open the app, it asks for your age group (for example 13–17 or 18–24). This answer is saved on your device only, to adapt the content and the comparison figures shown at the end of a game. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app.
  • Battery level. The app may read your battery level and charging status on the device to display a short in-game message. This information is not saved or sent anywhere.

3. Sharing your result

At the end of a game, you can choose to share an image of your result. The image is created on your device and handed to your phone's standard share menu. Nothing is shared unless you choose to do so, and the app does not see where you send it.

4. Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

5. App stores

Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) may collect information when you download the app, such as download counts, under their own privacy policies. We only receive the general download statistics they provide.

6. Children

Le Pouce is intended for people aged 13 and over. Because the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
