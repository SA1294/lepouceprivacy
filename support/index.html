Le Pouce – Support

Le Pouce is a mobile game about endless scrolling: a feed of videos that slowly empties out. The game only ends when you stop scrolling.

Contact us

For any question, bug report or feedback, email us at lucas@waffle-software.com. Please tell us your phone model and system version (iOS or Android) if you are reporting a problem.

Frequently asked questions

How do I end a game?
Stop scrolling. After a few seconds without touching the screen, the game ends and shows your results.

Is there an end to the feed?
No. There is nothing hidden at the end. That is the point of the game.

How do I change my age group?
Your age group is saved only on your phone. To choose it again, uninstall and reinstall the app.

Does the app collect my data?
Not while you play: the game collects nothing and does not use the internet. Only if you choose to make an optional donation, the purchase is handled by Apple or Google and recorded anonymously by our payment provider, RevenueCat. See our privacy policy.

The phone does not vibrate on Android.
Vibrations follow your phone's “Haptic feedback” setting. Turn it on in your phone's sound settings.
